# Reading list

Things the team read, or meant to read, while building Plexar Notes. Add your own. If a book changed how we built something, say which note it changed.

## Read

- **The Art of Unix Programming**, Eric Raymond. The "text streams are the universal interface" argument is why the app works on plain files and nothing else. See [[Plexar Notes]].
- **CommonMark spec**, John MacFarlane and others. We kept it open the whole time we wrote the [[Markdown cheatsheet]].
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications**, Martin Kleppmann. Only the chapter on encoding. Reminded us not to invent a file format.
- **Refactoring UI**, Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger. Where the spacing scale in the tokens file came from.

## Reading now

- **Working in Public**, Nadia Eghbal. Relevant because the whole build is on the record.
- **A Philosophy of Software Design**, John Ousterhout. Deep modules, shallow interfaces. The tree builder in the app is a fair example.
