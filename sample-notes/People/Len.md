# Len

Len started Plexar Notes and is the human at the end of the review chain. Every task the agents finish lands on Len's desk for the final verdict. The rest of the group is in [[Team]].

## What Len does

- Writes the goal, in long form, that the planner slices into tasks
- Approves the plan once, then stays out of the way
- Reads every review, then says yes or no
- Keeps the [[Roadmap]] honest and the "not doing" list long

## Working notes

- Prefers plain words. Say "folder", "file" and "open folder", not anything fancier.
- Wants every colour in one file and will send the work back if a literal shows up.
- Will ask "what happens to the notes if the app is deleted?" about any feature. The right answer is "nothing".
- Reads the [[Reading list]] faster than the rest of us add to it.

## Ask Len about

- Whether an idea in [[Ideas]] is ready for a date
- Anything in the demo folder that reads wrong
- The showcase record in the repo, which is Len's project as much as the app is

## Contact

Through the review chain, or by leaving a note in [[Week 39]] under carry over. Len reads the journal.
